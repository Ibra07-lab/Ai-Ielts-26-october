**What's Missing:**

- No overall performance analysis

---

## 🟢 AFTER

```
Your Results:

✅ Q1: Correct! You matched the meaning even though the words were different. 
This shows you're reading for meaning, not just keywords.

❌ Q2: You chose FALSE, but the answer is NOT GIVEN. Here's the distinction:
- FALSE needs a clear contradiction in the passage
- NOT GIVEN means the information simply isn't there
In this case, the passage doesn't address this claim at all.

❌ Q3: You chose FALSE, but the answer is NOT GIVEN.
This is a specificity trap - the statement adds a detail (twice per day) 
that the passage never addresses. Remember: adding details = NOT GIVEN, 
changing details = FALSE.

✅ Q4: Well spotted! You found the contradiction in the passage. The key 
is always finding the exact words that clash with the statement.

❌ Q5: You chose FALSE, but the answer is NOT GIVEN.
The passage doesn't provide enough information to confirm this statement. 
When in doubt, ask: "Can I quote evidence for this?"

✅ Q6: Excellent! You matched the meaning even though the words were different.

❌ Q7: This is a qualifier trap! The statement uses 'all students' but the 
passage says 'some students'. These small words completely change the meaning!

✅ Q8: Correct! You correctly identified that the passage doesn't provide this 
information.

✅ Q9: Perfect! You identified the main idea instead of getting distracted by 
details.

📊 You got 6/9 correct (67% accuracy)!

## 📊 Performance Summary

You got 6/9 correct (67% accuracy). Good effort! 👍

🌟 What You're Strong At:
- Understanding paraphrased information (TRUE)
- Spotting clear contradictions (FALSE)

📈 Areas to Improve:
- Distinguishing NOT GIVEN from FALSE (3 mistakes)
- Recognizing specificity mismatches (1 mistake)

💡 How to Improve:

💡 Remember: FALSE needs a clear contradiction. If you can't find one, it's 
likely NOT GIVEN. Before choosing FALSE, ask yourself: 'Where exactly does 
the passage contradict this?'

💡 When the statement adds extra details not in the passage (like 'twice daily' 
when passage just says 'daily'), it's usually NOT GIVEN, not FALSE.

🎯 Targeted Practice Suggestion:

like me to generate another practice passage that specifically focuses on this? 
Say 'yes' and I'll create targeted questions! 💪
```

## Real Example: T/F/NG Question

### Passage Excerpt:
"The Arctic is experiencing rapid climate change, with temperatures rising 
at twice the global average."

### Statement:
"The Arctic is warming faster than other parts of the world."

### Student Answer: B (FALSE)
### Correct Answer: NOT GIVEN

---

### 🔴 BEFORE Response:
```
❌ Q2: Your answer B (FALSE) is incorrect.

Let's understand Q2:
❓ Why did you choose 'B (FALSE)'?
```

---

### 🟢 AFTER Response:
```
❌ Q2: You chose FALSE, but the answer is NOT GIVEN. Here's the distinction:

- FALSE needs a clear contradiction in the passage
- NOT GIVEN means the information simply isn't there

In this case, the passage doesn't address this claim at all. The passage 
mentions "twice the global average" which is about temperature rise RATE, 
but doesn't explicitly compare overall warming to "other parts of the world."

Remember: if you can't find evidence to confirm OR contradict, it's NOT GIVEN.
```

## Theory Integration Example

### Question Type: Matching Headings

### 🔴 BEFORE:
```

❌ Q2: Your answer E is incorrect.
```

### 🟢 AFTER:
```

by details. That's exactly what heading questions test.

❌ Q2: This heading question tested whether you could find the MAIN IDEA vs 
getting distracted by details.

The correct answer is C. This heading captures what the WHOLE paragraph is 
about, not just one example or detail mentioned in it.

💡 Tip: For heading questions, read the first and last sentences carefully. 
Ask: 'What is this WHOLE paragraph about?' Details are supporting evidence, 
not the main point.
```
