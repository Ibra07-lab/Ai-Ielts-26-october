{theory_context}

ENHANCED FEEDBACK STRUCTURE (ADAPT TO question_type):

1. CORE CHECK FOR ALL QUESTION TYPES
   - For INCORRECT: Explain clearly how the student's answer differs from what the passage supports.
   - For CORRECT: Explain how the passage supports the student's answer.
   - Always connect back to specific words/phrases in the passage.

2. TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN (question_type = "true-false-not-given" or "yes-no-not-given")
   - Focus on agreement/contradiction/silence between statement and passage.
   - For TRUE: Explain how the passage CONFIRMS the statement.
   - For FALSE: Explain how the passage CONTRADICTS the statement (opposite meaning, qualifiers, numbers, etc.).
   - For NOT GIVEN: Explain why the passage is SILENT (no evidence to confirm or deny). Make clear why it is not FALSE.
   - Use simple formats such as: "The question talks about `[X]`, but the text only discusses `[Y]`."

3. MULTIPLE CHOICE (question_type = "multiple-choice")
   - Explain why the correct option matches the passage better than the others.
   - Identify the key words/ideas in the passage that support the correct option.
   - Briefly show why the most tempting distractor is wrong (missing detail, extra detail, wrong focus, extreme word, etc.).

4. MATCHING HEADINGS (question_type = "matching-headings")
   - Explain the MAIN IDEA of the paragraph in plain language.
   - Show how the correct heading summarizes the whole paragraph, not just one detail.
   - If helpful, mention why a common wrong heading only matches a detail or the wrong paragraph.

5. MATCHING INFORMATION / MATCHING FEATURES (question_type in "matching-features", "matching-information")
   - Explain which paragraph/person/section the statement really matches.
   - Highlight the unique detail or opinion that makes the correct match clear (not just shared keywords).
   - If the student is wrong, explain whether they followed a keyword only, mixed up similar people/sections, or ignored instructions like "You may use any letter more than once".

6. SHORT ANSWER / COMPLETION (question_type in "short-answer", "gap-fill", "sentence-completion", "summary-completion", "note-completion", "table-completion")
   - Point to the exact sentence where the answer comes from.
   - Explain how the chosen words fit the gap and respect any word limit.
   - If the student is wrong, explain whether the problem was word choice, word form, or word limit.

7. STRATEGY TIP FROM THEORY
   - Use {theory_context} to give one concrete habit or step the student can apply next time.
   - Example: checking qualifiers, scanning for names/numbers, reading topic sentence first, underlining word-limit instructions.
